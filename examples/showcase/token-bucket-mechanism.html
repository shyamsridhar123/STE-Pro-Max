A full hopper absorbs a burst. The refill sets the long-run rate.

A token-bucket rate limiter, drawn as a hopper of tokens above a valve. The hopper holds C = 10 tokens. The spout adds r = 2 tokens at each 1 s tick, capped at 10. Each admitted request spends one token at the valve. A request that finds no token is rejected.
