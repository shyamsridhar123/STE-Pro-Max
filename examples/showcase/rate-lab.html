# Grounded explanation

Success increased by 2.3 percentage points, from 97.4% to 99.7%.
The relative increase was about 2.36%. The cause is not established.

Percentage-point change is the final rate minus the starting rate.
Relative change divides that difference by the starting rate, then multiplies by 100.
A starting rate of zero makes this relative comparison undefined.

Inputs change only this local calculation. They do not change the source. For edited values, percentage-point results are shown to at most six decimal places; relative results to two. Tiny nonzero changes are labelled rather than shown as zero.
