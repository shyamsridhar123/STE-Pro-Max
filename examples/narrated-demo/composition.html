Change is not a cause.

One example. Two measures. A clear limit.

97.4%STARTING RATE
→
99.7%FINAL RATE
ABSOLUTE CHANGE
+2.3 percentage points
99.7 − 97.4 = 2.3
RELATIVE CHANGE
≈2.36%
(2.3 ÷ 97.4) × 100

The cause is not established.
