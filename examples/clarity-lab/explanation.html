01 / An explanation you can test

The numbers changed.
What can we actually claim?

A percentage point is not a percent change. Change the example, compare the two measures, and keep the cause separate from the observation.

Set the example

Use rates from 0 to 100, in steps of 0.1. These are illustrative values, not live data.

Compare the rates

Absolute change
+2.3 pp
99.7 − 97.4 = 2.3 percentage points
Relative change
+2.36%
2.3 ÷ 97.4 × 100 ≈ 2.36%
Change is an observation. Cause needs evidence.

No causal explanation is supplied. A chart, calculation, or animation does not establish why the rate changed.
